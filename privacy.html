Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy describes how Clipperino Poster ("the App") handles information.

Information the App uses

When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok provides an access token that the App stores on the server where it is hosted. The App uses this token only to publish videos that you choose to schedule to that account.

Information we do not collect

The App does not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers or other third parties. It does not use TikTok data for advertising or profiling.

Data storage

The App runs on infrastructure controlled by its operator. Videos and tokens stay on that infrastructure, except for the videos sent to TikTok when you publish.

Your choices

You can disconnect a TikTok account in the App at any time. You can also revoke the App's access from your TikTok account settings.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at this repository's Issues page.
